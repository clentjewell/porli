# Higgsfield image direction

Use these prompts through the available Higgsfield image-generation capability when imagery is requested. Do not submit generations simply by importing the repository. Check tool requirements and any billing choice returned at execution time. Maintain an asset register with prompt, model, generation ID, date, local filename, purpose and fictional designation.

## Shared direction
Editorial architectural photography, natural restrained colours, believable scale, straight verticals, tactile materials, slight lived-in detail, no people required, no text/logos, no CGI sheen. Maintain warm ivory, foliage greens and timber tones. Produce landscape images with safe card crops. Match location/property facts if using a fictional fixture.

## Image 1 — Brand mood / courtyard
A modest architect-renovated coastal courtyard house, viewed obliquely from a shaded veranda through a timber doorway into a sunlit courtyard. Pale limewashed brick, weathered timber, olive foliage, one worn chair, linen curtain. Mid-morning raking light, deep olive shadows, subtle film grain. Asymmetrical magazine composition, natural 35mm perspective. No mansion, pool, sunset, aerial view or text. Fictional concept property.

## Image 2 — Apartment
A compact two-bedroom apartment living area opening to a planted balcony, honest proportions, oak shelves, pale plaster, linen sofa, one book left on the table. Soft overcast daylight, restrained editorial styling, straight verticals. No city landmark or impossible view. Fictional concept property.

## Image 3 — Townhouse
A contemporary brick townhouse entrance with a small front garden, tactile brickwork, olive-painted door and late-morning side light. Eye-level street perspective with modest scale and realistic neighbouring context. No signs, numbers, logos or people. Fictional concept property.

## Image 4 — Family home
A welcoming single-storey family home with a practical garden and covered outdoor space. Natural timber, neutral brick, mature planting and believable wear. Bright soft daylight, restrained composition, no oversized luxury features. Fictional concept property.

## Usage
Generated imagery belongs in prototype/demo content or clearly labelled brand studies. It must not substitute for photographs of an actual listed home. Multiple rooms advertised as one fictional property should have consistent architecture; use references where supported rather than inventing unrelated interiors.

A courtyard study was generated earlier in the concept conversation under the old working title Porch & Key. This package does not embed or depend on that externally hosted file; these prompts are sufficient to create a coherent Porli image set. No final logo or finished website mock-up is included.
